BEHIND THE GRILL

Meet Chef Sami

Sami grew up in Karachi, watching his father grill fish with nothing but salt, lemon, and a handful of coals. At Ember & Salt he still cooks that way, ordering what looks good at the market and letting the fire do most of the work.

His favourite seat is by the pass, close enough to watch the flatbreads puff up over the coals.
